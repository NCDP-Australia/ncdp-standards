The **National Coastal Drone Program (NCDP)** is a nationally coordinated archive of UAV coastal survey
data, coordinated by Deakin University and funded by AuScope through NCRIS. These pages are its data
standards and procedures: how surveys are structured, named and described, how to contribute one, how
submissions are checked, and how the coastal change indicators and site reports are produced.

This wiki shows the **locked revision**. Drafts for the next revision are edited in the
[repository](https://github.com/NCDP-Australia/ncdp-standards); see its README for how to propose a change.
Data and maps: [ncdp.auscope.org.au](https://ncdp.auscope.org.au).
